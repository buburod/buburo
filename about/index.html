Bruno Rodrigues

I’m a product designer who writes the code. Fourteen years in the tech industry, most of it on products where the distance between a design decision and a shipped build is short — which is where I think the interesting problems are.

Based in Lisbon. I run Buburo, where I take product ideas from concept to working applications, and I’ve been part of Agroop since 2013 — first as designer and co-founder, more recently building the platform itself.
